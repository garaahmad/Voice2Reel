# Dataset provenance

`script` records are **assistant-authored synthetic starter examples**, created for this repository on 2026-11-06. They were not scraped from publishers or generated by a measured teacher-model labeling pipeline, and have not received independent expert review.

- `scripts/train.jsonl`: 30 ideas and structured answers.
- `scripts/validation.jsonl`: 5 distinct topics.
- `scripts/test.jsonl`: 5 distinct topics held out for final comparisons.
- All examples currently use Modern Standard Arabic, 20-second targets, and three narration beats. This narrow distribution is intentional for a small end-to-end training experiment; it does not represent the full supported schema or all Arabic dialects.
- `voice/recording_prompts.csv`: 120 sentence prompts derived from the authored narration.
- `voice/manifest.template.jsonl`: matching filenames/text/speaker identifiers; audio files are **not included**.
- `voice/evaluation_texts.txt`: separate prompts for listening tests.

Record your own audio, remove missing entries from the manifest, and add more varied material. Artificial tones generated by unit tests are test fixtures only and are never included in the training dataset. Training/validation/test splits are checked for duplicate IDs, ideas, and narration. Voice preparation also detects duplicate audio and text before splitting.

Record any new dataset's source, labeling method, license, speaker composition, and held-out split policy before mixing it with these examples.
